Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 3, 2026

Iron CRM ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal training business management app. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of data:

  • Account information: Your name, email address, and password when you create an account. If you use Sign in with Apple, we receive your name and email (or a relay address) from Apple.
  • Business data: Client records, session/appointment details, payment records, notes, and follow-ups that you enter into the app.
  • Device identifier: A vendor-specific device identifier used solely to prevent trial abuse. This identifier is not used for tracking or advertising.
  • Crash and performance data: Anonymized crash reports and performance metrics collected via Sentry to help us fix bugs and improve stability. These reports may include your user ID for debugging purposes.
  • Subscription data: Purchase and subscription status managed through Apple's App Store and RevenueCat.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the app's core CRM functionality.
  • To authenticate your identity and secure your account.
  • To manage your subscription and process payments through the App Store.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app performance.
  • To send local push notifications for appointment reminders (only with your permission).

3. Data Storage and Security

Your business data is stored locally on your device using an encrypted SQLite database and synced to our cloud servers hosted on Supabase (powered by AWS). Data in transit is encrypted via TLS. Data at rest is encrypted by the hosting provider.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Authentication and cloud database hosting.
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management and purchase validation.
  • Sentry: Error tracking and crash reporting.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple authentication and in-app purchase processing.

These services have their own privacy policies. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing.

5. Data Tracking

Iron CRM does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not use advertising identifiers. We do not participate in ad networks.

6. Calendar Access

If you grant calendar permission, the app creates a dedicated "Iron CRM Sessions" calendar on your device to sync training session schedules. We do not read or access your other calendars.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from Settings > Account > Delete Account. When you delete your account, all associated data (clients, sessions, payments, notes) is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Iron CRM is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us or using the in-app account deletion feature.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at support@ironcrm.app.
